Railway Gate Tracker

Drivers in Kerala lose twenty minutes at a level crossing they could have driven around. RGT takes a road route, finds every crossing on it, and works out which ones a train is about to close.

V1 scraped the National Train Enquiry System with Selenium, which was slow and broke whenever the page moved. V2 replaced it with the RailRadar REST API, matched crossings to the route by haversine distance, and predicted gate occupancy from train ETAs instead of guessing.

PlayON

A World Cup prediction platform I designed, shipped and operated alone, through all 104 fixtures.

Players

Six game modes ran off one Postgres scoring engine: a recompute_scores() function fired by a pg_cron heartbeat after each fixture sync. Deadlines and prediction privacy were enforced in the database with Row Level Security and SECURITY DEFINER RPCs, so no client could leak the crowd's picks before kickoff. With no budget for a live-sports API, a browser userscript scraped match pages and posted results in, scoring fantasy standings mid-match.

React 19 · Supabase (Postgres, RLS, Deno Edge Functions) · Vite · Vercel

Session Gist

Record or upload a mentorship session and get back the words it was actually about.

Groq transcribes the audio, a second model weights the terms the session dwelt on, and the result renders as an SVG word cloud you can download. Limits are stated before you wait, and a denied microphone or an oversized file fails with a message naming the browser you are in, not a console error.

TypeScript · Cloudflare Workers · Groq

WACA Predict

A stdlib-only parser that reads hand-kept cricket scorecards out of raw WhatsApp exports.

Free text, emoji and inconsistent naming turned into 895 matches, 1,264 players and more than 38,000 ball-by-ball events. Player identities were resolved across aliases and nicknames, then shipped as a dependency-free single-file HTML dashboard with phone numbers masked in the public build.
